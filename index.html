About

Hi! I’m Vera, a Game Producer with a background in Creative Technologies and a Master’s degree in Game Development from Abertay University.
I’m passionate about helping bring a team together to bring games to life!

I have experience working across game development pipelines, having served as a producer on four games, three of which are available on itch.io (you can find out more below). My strengths include teamwork, team management, project management, interpersonal communication, and organization.

As a producer, I pride myself in looking to find the right methods and processes that’ll serve the team and the project. For this purpose, I love learning more about people, production processes and games!

Based in Dundee, Scotland
